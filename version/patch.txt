# Patch Notes

## Fixed
- Fixed messy output caused by rate limiting.

## New

### Latency warning
A warning now appears when response latency exceeds 1000ms to let you know responses may slow down. Can be disabled in developer settings.

### Webhook notifier improvements
Refreshed embed style for on-sale, on-sold, and verification events. Each event now has its own dedicated ping option so you're only notified for conditions you care about. Pings can be disabled individually.

### `skip_on_sale_persistent`
A persistent skip that reads from `onmarker.json` instead of relying solely on the API. Even if your local data is reset, items already listed for resale won't be re-sold.

### `skip_on_sale`
A volatile but more accurate API-based skip. Resets with the session  best paired with `skip_on_sale_persistent` for full coverage.

### `skip_assetId`
Prevents specific items from being listed for sale by asset ID.
